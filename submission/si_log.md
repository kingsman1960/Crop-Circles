Scores are rule-based and a transparent model – no trained model. Every number in the app comes from the pipeline and the datasets in `content/sources.toml`. The optional *Ask about this field* box (`app/copilot.py`) is the one LLM at runtime: it receives the page's own sentences and figures as a fact card, is told to compute nothing, and every number in its answer that is not on the card is listed under the answer; without an API key it is off.
