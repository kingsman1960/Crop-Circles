## 1. Crop rotations on Kraichgau loess slopes and soil erosion

- [Einzelbetrieblicher Nutzen-Kosten-Vergleich von Erosionsschutz durch Mulchsaatverfahren](https://www.openagrar.de/receive/timport_mods_00031803) – Brunotte, Roth, Hollmann, Sommer; Landbauforschung Völkenrode 45(3):122–134 (FAL, now Thünen; OpenAgrar), 1995 – Farm-level cost-benefit study of mulch seeding for sugar beet on a model farm with a sugar beet → winter wheat → winter barley (+ cover crop) rotation. The search index of the PDF says the model site (10 % slope, loess Parabraunerde) is described as representative of loess areas including the Kraichgau. That wording is not on the record page. Full-text PDF linked from the record: https://www.openagrar.de/servlets/MCRFileNodeServlet/timport_derivate_00049314/Brunotte_et_al_pp_122_ff.pdf
  - Notes:
- [Mulchsaat gegen Erosion: worauf es bei Zuckerrüben ankommt](https://www.openagrar.de/receive/timport_mods_00024189) – Brunotte, Schäfer, Wollenweber; BW agrar 60(12):19–21 (Thünen record on OpenAgrar), 2008 – The abstract says mulch seeding has worked against crusting and erosion "in erosionsgefährdeten Gebieten wie im Kraichgau" and lists rules for sowing sugar beet. Full text restricted; metadata only.
  - Notes:
- [Do rotations with cover crops increase yield and soil organic carbon?—A modeling study in southwest Germany](https://www.openagrar.de/receive/openagrar_mods_00098896) – Attia, Marohn, Shawon, de Kock, Strassemeyer, Feike; Agriculture, Ecosystems & Environment (OpenAgrar, CC BY-NC-ND), 2024 – DSSAT model calibrated with 10 years of data from six sites in southwest Germany. Compares a 4-year rotation with and without legume and non-legume cover crops: SOC, soil N, N leaching, yields. DOI 10.1016/j.agee.2024.109167.

## 2. Reed planthopper (Schilf-Glasflügelzikade, Pentastiridius leporinus) and rotation advice

### JKI (Julius Kühn-Institut)

- [Regionale Maßnahmenempfehlung zur Schilf-Glasflügelzikade](https://www.julius-kuehn.de/a/aktuelles-a/n/regionale-massnahmenempfehlung-zur-schilf-glasfluegelzikade) – JKI Institut für Pflanzenschutz in Ackerbau und Grünland with the state plant protection services, 2025 (12.05.2025) – Three region types: hot-spot (>50 % of plants infected), transition (10–50 %), border (<10 %). Rotation change is mandatory in hot spots, e.g. no winter wheat after sugar beet or potato. In border regions, rotation adjustment is the main measure.
  - Notes:
- [Schilf-Glasflügelzikade: Neue Strategien gegen ein wachsendes Risiko (Kühn konkret)](https://www.julius-kuehn.de/infothek/kuehn-konkret/schilf-glasfluegelzikade) – JKI, undated (current page) – JKI topic page on biology, SBR/Stolbur and the three-region model. Calls for "abgestimmte Strategien über gesamte Fruchtfolgen in Raum und Zeit". Links to a publication list and a flyer.
  - Notes:
- [Controlling the planthopper Pentastiridius leporinus using practical methods](https://www.julius-kuehn.de/en/news/nachricht/n/schilf-glasfluegelzikade-mit-praxisnahen-mitteln-bekaempfen) – JKI, 2025 (24.10.2025) – English news item on the Elbaue model region (about 2,000 ha): more than 75 % of the area taken out of winter crops (black fallow), GLÖZ 6 exemptions, emergency insecticide authorisations.
  - Notes:
- [Schilf-Glasflügelzikade (Faltblatt)](https://www.julius-kuehn.de/media/KuehnKonkret/Glasfluegelzikade/pdf-mp4/UNIKA_Faltblatt_Schilf-Glasfluegelzikade_WEB.pdf) – UNIKA with JKI Dossenheim / GeKaPent project, year not shown – Flyer for growers (PDF, focus on potato), linked from the JKI topic page. Content not reviewed.
  - Notes:
- [Wintertagung Groitzsch, 04.12.2025 – Vortrag Hausmann (JKI)](https://landwirtschaft.sachsen.de/download/2025-12-04_Wintertagung_Groitzsch_Hausmann_Freigabe.pdf) – JKI talk hosted by Saxony's agriculture portal, 2025 – According to search snippets: model regions in southern Germany with no winter crops after sugar beet, preliminary rotation results, and planthopper emergence in wheat after beet vs. maize. Descriptive title taken from the file name; content not reviewed.

### LIZ (Landwirtschaftlicher Informationsdienst Zuckerrübe)

- [SBR und Stolbur erklärt – Teil 5: Bekämpfungsmöglichkeiten der Schilf-Glasflügelzikade](https://www.liz-online.de/meldungen/meldung-ueberregional/sbr-und-stolbur-erklaert-teil-5-bekaempfungsmoeglichkeiten-der-schilf-glasfluegelzikade) – LIZ, 2025 (17.10.2025) – Calls rotation "die aktuell effektivste Maßnahme". Dropping winter cereals (Rübenweizen) after sugar beet cut nymph populations by up to 90 % in model regions in southern and eastern Germany. Early inverting tillage helps while the soil is still warm.
  - Notes:
- [SBR und Stolbur erklärt – Teil 2: Die Biologie und der Lebenszyklus der Schilf-Glasflügelzikade & der Erreger](https://www.liz-online.de/meldungen/meldung-ueberregional/sbr-und-stolbur-erklaert-teil-2-die-biologie-und-der-lebenszyklus-der-schilf-glasfluegelzikade-der-erreger) – LIZ, 2025 (19.09.2025) – Life cycle: eggs laid at the beet in the soil; nymphs feed on beet roots, then on the following winter wheat, and overwinter 40–50 cm deep. Adults fly from the wheat to nearby beet fields.
  - Notes:
- [SBR und Stolbur erklärt – Teil 1: Herkunft und Verbreitung der Schilf-Glasflügelzikade](https://www.liz-online.de/meldungen/meldung-ueberregional/sbr-und-stolbur-erklaert-teil-1-herkunft-und-verbreitung-der-schilf-glasfluegelzikade) – LIZ, 2025 (12.09.2025) – Origin and spread; says beet growing in southern Germany is hit hardest. The series has six parts (Teil 3: symptoms, Teil 4: nationwide monitoring).
  - Notes:
- [SBR und Stolbur erklärt – Teil 6: Forschungsansätze und zukünftige Lösungsmöglichkeiten](https://www.liz-online.de/meldungen/meldung-ueberregional/sbr-und-stolbur-erklaert-teil-6-forschungsansaetze-und-zukuenftige-loesungsmoeglichkeiten) – LIZ, 2025 (31.10.2025) – Research outlook, including when Rübenweizen might stay in the rotation.
  - Notes:
- [SBR-Krankheitskomplex – Niedrige Zuckergehalte & Gummirüben (Ausgabe 2026)](https://www.liz-online.de/fileadmin/user_upload/pdf/Syndrome_Basses_Richesses/SyndromeBassesRichesses.pdf) – LIZ (photos credited to LLG Sachsen-Anhalt and IfZ), 2026 – Info sheet (PDF). Prevention: grow non-host crops after beet (pea, maize), avoid winter cereals after beet; fallow gives the strongest population cut. Also covers symptoms and monitoring. LIZ menu link: https://www.liz-online.de/ruebenanbau/krankheiten-schaeden/default-title
  - Notes:

## 3. Soybean in Baden-Württemberg and nitrogen for the following crop

- [Soja: Der Star von morgen?](https://www.topagrar.com/acker/aus-dem-heft/soja-der-star-von-morgen-9678436.html) – top agrar, 2012 (23.07.2012) – (paywall) Says the following crop gets about 30–50 kg N/ha from soybean residues. Names the Oberrheingraben as suitable for 00 varieties.
  - Notes:
- [Alternative Kulturen: Was die Sojabohne leistet](https://www.topagrar.com/acker/news/das-leistet-die-sojabohne-13110200.html) – top agrar, 2022 (12.06.2022) – (paywall) About three quarters of Germany's 35,000 ha of soybean (2021) were in Bavaria and Baden-Württemberg. Describes soy as using up the N it fixes, unlike pea and faba bean.
  - Notes:
- [Fruchtfolge (Anbauratgeber)](https://sojafoerderring.de/anbauratgeber/fruchtfolge/) – Deutscher Sojaförderring e.V., undated – Rates soy's preceding-crop value below faba bean and pea, but the following crop can still save about 30–50 kg N/ha. A 3-year break is standard; maize is a good rotation partner.
  - Notes:
- [Optimierung des Anbaus von Sojabohnen: Bestimmung des Vorfruchtwertes und der N2-Fixierleistung sowie Reduzierung der Bodenbearbeitung (FixVorSaat Soja)](https://orgprints.org/id/eprint/36906/) – Butz (LTZ Augustenberg, Rheinstetten-Forchheim), Paeßens, Urbatzka (LfL Bayern); Organic Eprints, 2019 – Final report record with practice leaflets. Covers N2 fixation and N balance (more N removed with grain than fixed) and the effect on the following winter wheat: no yield difference after soy vs. pea in conventional farming, peas better in organic.
  - Notes:
- [Praxisblatt 02: Sojabohnen – N2-Fixierung (FixVorSaat Soja)](https://www.orgprints.org/id/eprint/36906/6/14EPS019_14EPS020_Pflanzenbau_Sojabohnen_N2-Fixierung.pdf) – LTZ Augustenberg / LfL Bayern, 2019 – Practice leaflet (PDF) linked from the record above. Search snippets give 50 to more than 100 kg N/ha in soy residues. Descriptive title; content not reviewed.
  - Notes:
